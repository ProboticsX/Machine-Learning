- Sean 'Diddy' Combs Trial Update: The high-profile trial of hip-hop mogul Sean "Diddy" Combs wrapped up its first week in Manhattan federal court amid charges including sex trafficking, racketeering conspiracy and transportation to engage in prostitution. Former girlfriend Cassie Ventura, the prosecution's star witness, testified for over 20 hours, describing a decade-long relationship marked by physical abuse and drug-fueled sexual encounters called "freak offs." The defense presented texts suggesting Ventura was a willing participant. Additional witnesses including a Homeland Security agent and singer Dawn Richard testified, the latter recounting witnessing Combs assault Ventura. If convicted, Combs faces life imprisonment and is currently held without bail. The trial is expected to last at least eight weeks. This case has also seen outreach by Combs's allies to Trump officials for a pardon, underscoring the ongoing political dynamics surrounding it.
