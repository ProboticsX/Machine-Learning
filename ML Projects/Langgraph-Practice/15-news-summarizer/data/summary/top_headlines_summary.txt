- Sean 'Diddy' Combs trial live updates: Cassie Ventura Returns to Witness Stand

The ongoing federal trial against hip-hop mogul Sean 'Diddy' Combs, charged with sex trafficking, racketeering conspiracy, and related offenses, features detailed witness testimony from his former partner Cassie Ventura. Prosecutors presented a 2016 video of Combs physically assaulting Ventura and testimonies from witnesses including singer Dawn Richard who reported seeing abuse. The trial is held in Manhattan federal court with jury deliberations expected after an extensive multi-week hearing. If convicted, Combs faces potential life imprisonment.

- Salman Rushdie Attack: Hadi Matar Sentenced to 25 Years

Hadi Matar was sentenced to the maximum 25 years in prison following his conviction for attempted murder in the 2022 stabbing attack on author Salman Rushdie. The assault during a public lecture caused severe injuries, including permanent nerve damage and partial blindness for Rushdie. The attack is connected to a fatwa issued in 1989 calling for Rushdie's death over his controversial novel "The Satanic Verses." Matar also faces federal terrorism charges with a separate trial pending. Rushdie did not attend the sentencing but submitted a victim impact statement. The sentencing underscores ongoing tensions related to extremist threats and freedom of expression.

- Chris Brown Held in Custody Over London Nightclub Assault Charge

Grammy-winning artist Chris Brown was remanded in custody by a UK court following charges related to an assault at a London nightclub in 2023, where he allegedly severely beat a music producer. The judge denied bail citing the seriousness of the offense. Brown's next court hearing is scheduled in June, coinciding with his planned European tour dates, casting uncertainty over his international performance schedule. This case adds to the artist's well-documented history of legal troubles and public controversies.
